\chapter{Thermodynamic Foundations of Macromolecular Systems}
\label{ch:thermodynamics}

The macroscopic state of a thermodynamic system at equilibrium is governed by the fundamental state functions of internal energy $U$, temperature $T$, and entropy $S$. In biological macromolecules, conformational stability is parameterized by the standard Gibbs free energy change:

\begin{equation}
  \Delta G^\circ = \Delta H^\circ - T \Delta S^\circ
  \label{eq:gibbs}
\end{equation}

Where $\Delta G^\circ$ specifies the free energy change relative to standard state reference concentrations. A negative value indicates a spontaneous, thermodynamically favorable transition toward the folded state.
